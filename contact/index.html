Shipping & Delivery Policy

Complimentary Express Delivery: All prepaid and Cash-on-Delivery orders above ₹999 qualify for complimentary shipping across 19,000+ Indian pin codes. Orders below ₹999 incur a flat ₹99 delivery fee.

Dispatch & Transit Timelines: Ready-to-ship garments are quality-checked, steam-finished, and dispatched within 24–48 business hours. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days for metro cities and 5–7 business days for rest of India.

Order Tracking: Once your parcel is handed over to our courier partner, you will receive an email confirmation with your live tracking link and AWB reference number.

7-Day Exchanges & Returns Policy

Easy 7-Day Window: We offer hassle-free size exchanges and returns within 7 days of delivery. You can initiate a request directly from your My Account dashboard or via your Order Confirmation page.

Eligibility Conditions: Garments must be unused, unwashed, and returned in their original packaging with all Matra Naari tags intact.

Complimentary Reverse Pickup: Our courier partner will arrange a doorstep pickup within 48–72 hours of request approval. Replacement sizes are dispatched immediately upon quality inspection, or refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

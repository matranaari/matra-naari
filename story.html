our story

Woven with meaning

The name

Naari means woman — strong, graceful, and endlessly herself.

Two scripts, one soul

Devanagari and Roman letters meet in our logo, just as tradition meets today in our designs.

The craft

Every kurti is cut for comfort and finished with care, by hands that love what they make.

For you

Made for the office, the festival, the café and everything in between.

Naari was born from a wardrobe full of kurtis we loved — and a longing for ones that felt modern without losing their roots.

So we started making them ourselves: clean silhouettes, breathable fabrics, and little details that whisper of home. Today, every Naari kurti carries that same promise.

Modern kurtis, woven with heritage.
